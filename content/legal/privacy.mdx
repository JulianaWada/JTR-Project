## Which laws apply

We aim to comply with the data protection laws that apply to the people we work with, including:

- the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**;
- the **UK GDPR** and the UK Data Protection Act 2018;
- the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** and guidance from the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## What we collect

- **Enquiry details** you send through our contact form or AI assistant: name, email, phone, company, country, time zone, service of interest, budget range and your message.
- **Assistant conversations**: the messages you exchange with the JTR Assistant, processed to generate replies.
- **Technical data**: IP address (used briefly for security and rate limiting) and basic request information.
- **Analytics data**, only if you accept analytics cookies.

## Why we use it (legal bases)

- To reply to your enquiry and take steps before entering a contract (**contract / pre-contract**).
- To keep the site secure and prevent abuse (**legitimate interests**).
- To understand how the site is used, only with your permission (**consent**).
